SCENE: Thirty seconds, seven shots, hard cuts. Infiltration as if danger were near. Seed and Rob1 must cross a plain at dusk where a herd of giant white animals sleeps like scattered hills. SHOTS (30 seconds, 7 shots, hard cuts):
0.0s to 4.0s, SHOT 1, wide, static. At the edge of the blue-grass valley, Seed and Rob1 stop and look out over a golden plain dotted with huge white mounds, sleeping giants, under a thin orange line of distant fire. Rob1 raises one flat black hand: stop.
HARD CUT.
4.0s to 9.0s, SHOT 2, medium shot. Hand signals: Rob1 points two fingers at his visor and then at the herd; Seed copies him; she presses a finger to her lips; his visor shows one thin cyan bar for silence. They tiptoe forward with exaggerated high knees, comically careful.
HARD CUT.
9.0s to 14.0s, SHOT 3, low wide shot, deep focus. They thread between sleeping giants: thick white wool rising and falling, enormous column legs, curved horns resting on the grass, warm breath rolling out like fog. The two tiny figures weave through them.

18.0s to 24.0s, SHOT 5, medium-wide shot. A giant rolls over in its sleep and a massive leg swings down beside them. HARD CUT.
24.0s to 27.0s, SHOT 6, close-up at eye level. One enormous eye half opens right in front of them. They freeze and hold their breath; Rob1's visor goes completely dark. The eye slowly closes again.
HARD CUT.
27.0s to 30.0s, SHOT 7, wide shot. They slip out past the last sleeper and collapse in the grass, Seed in a silent laughing fit, Rob1 giving a proud wink. Behind them the herd breathes slowly; the orange fire line glows on the horizon.
