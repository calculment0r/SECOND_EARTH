SHOTS (30 seconds, 7 shots, hard cuts):
0.0s to 5.0s, SHOT 1, wide shot, slow push-in. A pale pink plain dotted with red-rose mushroom plants and white stones. Seed, Rob1 and the big robot walk in a line toward the camera, Rob1 riding on the big robot's shoulders.
HARD CUT.
5.0s to 9.0s, SHOT 2, wide shot, low. A red clearing at dusk, thick red mist, a large red moon low in the sky. They reach the edge of the dark pit. Seed looks down; far below, the dented cream top of the capsule lies in the shadow.

9.0s to 14.0s, SHOT 3, medium shot. Rob1 hops down, his visor drawing a thin cyan angle line that tilts, then a second line, planning the pull. The big robot nods its lens. Seed ties a steel cable around the trunk of the crimson tree.

25.0s to 28.0s, SHOT 6, medium shot. The cable slips and Rob1 shoots backward and tumbles across the red grass, Seed grabs the cable with both hands, the big robot catches them both with one long arm.
